# Practice transcripts

These interviews are fictional. They exist so the field kit can be practiced without contacting a real claims office. Quotes in your evidence table should come from this file word for word. Northline Mutual, Lumen, and the handlers are invented.

Tag each quote you keep as observation, pain, workaround, or opinion. A few lines are opinions dressed up as facts. Leave them tagged as opinion.

## T1 — Priya Raman, handler, Columbus day shift, 4 years

Interviewer: Walk me through the last new auto claim you opened.

Priya: Yesterday, a parking-lot scrape in Dayton. I had the first notice in Lumen by 8:40. The date of loss was March 2. I knew the Ohio form changed in February, so I did not trust the form Lumen suggested.

Interviewer: What did you do next?

Priya: I opened the shared drive, folder "Personal Auto / OH," and sorted by name. There are two PDFs that both say "PP 00 01." One has "2024-11" in the filename and one has "2026-02." The February file is the one in force for a March loss. I copied the collision exclusion paragraph into the note, then I deleted it, because the bulletin on January 12 changed the rental limit and the PDF does not mention the bulletin.

Interviewer: Where does that bulletin live?

Priya: Email. I forward the weekly bulletin to myself with the subject "KEEP" and I paste the rental lines into a document on my desktop called `oh-auto-deltas`. Search in the shared drive does not find the bulletin. If I am out, the person covering my desk does not have that document.

Interviewer: What happened with the note?

Priya: My supervisor sent it back this morning. I had the right collision paragraph and the old rental limit. She wrote "use the January bulletin." That bounce cost me another twelve minutes, and the claimant had already called once asking why we had not confirmed the rental.

Interviewer: If you could change one thing about the tools, what would it be?

Priya: I would love a copilot. Everyone is talking about them. Honestly I just want the file that was actually in force, and I want it to show the bulletin on the same screen. I do not want it writing the coverage decision. I got written up two years ago for a sentence that sounded like I had denied a claim in the note.

## T2 — Andre Cole, handler, Columbus day shift, 11 months

Interviewer: Tell me about a time you were unsure which form applied.

Andre: Last Thursday. Hail claim, policy started in 2025, loss was last week. Lumen showed a form code I had never seen, `PP 03 02 OH 01 25`. I asked the handler next to me. She said "if Lumen shows it, it is fine." I did not like that, so I called the help desk. They said the library index is updated on Fridays. This was a Wednesday. So the index on my screen was five days old.

Interviewer: What did you put in the note?

Andre: I wrote "form per Lumen" and I did not paste any language. My supervisor added the paragraph herself and told me to stop leaving it blank, because blank notes fail the audit even when the form is right. I would rather be corrected than copy the wrong paragraph. I keep a list in my notebook of codes she has fixed. It is three pages now.

Interviewer: How long did that claim take?

Andre: The first pass was about twenty minutes. With her rewrite, the file sat until after lunch. The rental car company called her, not me, because the note never went out.

Interviewer: What would a good tool do here?

Andre: Something that just does the note for me so I can get to the next file. We are measured on closures. I am behind this week. If the tool is wrong sometimes, I can fix it at the end of the day.
